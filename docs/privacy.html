bank-sync — Privacy

bank-sync is a self-hosted tool that one person runs for their own bank accounts. There are no other users.

Account and transaction data fetched through Enable Banking is stored only on the operator’s own server. It is not shared, sold or sent to any third party, and is used only to track the operator’s own spending.

Access can be revoked at any time from the bank or from the tool itself; consent expires automatically after the period granted by the bank.

Source: github.com/maxmaxme/bank-sync
